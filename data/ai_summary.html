AI Nutrition Summary 2023 – 2026 · Delhaize · per 2 500 kcal bought · EU reference intakes

This describes the nutrient composition of the groceries bought, not what anyone ate: waste, pantry stock, meals eaten out and the Carrefour/Colruyt receipts are not included. Percentages compare the basket's nutrient density with the EU Regulation 1169/2011 adult reference intakes rescaled to 2 500 kcal; they are not a personal adequacy score. 125 receipts, 3 640 lines, 80 % matched to a food, 67 % matched and carrying a quantity (only those feed the figures). 2026 covers January to July.

✓ Strengths
  • Sugars below the reference (88 %) and mostly from fruit: apples, bananas and raisins lead, with chocolate-hazelnut spread the only processed source in the top five.
  • Sodium at the reference (94 %, 6 g salt equivalent). The earlier "3× sodium" figure came from a 1 500 mg reference that matched no published standard; bread, ham and pork remain the main sources.
  • Fibre climbing steadily: 69 % (2023) → 84 % → 97 % → 97 % of the EFSA adequate intake, carried by bread, bananas, carrots, avocados and apples.
  • Vitamin C, iron, folate, B vitamins and zinc all at or above the reference every year; vitamin C rose from 74 % to 117 %.
  • Protein at twice the reference (203 % of 62.5 g): meat, mozzarella, ham and hummus dominate the basket.
⚠ Watch
  • Saturated fat at 178 % of the reference, driven by cream (13 %), butter (12 %), mozzarella (11 %), beef and chocolate spread. It has fallen from 217 % in 2023 to 155–168 % in 2025–2026.
  • Vitamin D at 58 % of the reference. Eggs, pork and salmon are the main sources in the basket; this is also the nutrient with the lowest data coverage (90 %), so the figure is the least certain one here.
  • Magnesium at 78 %. The basket's magnesium comes mainly from bread, pork and bananas; nuts, legumes and leafy greens are a small share of purchases.
  • Carbohydrate at 81 % of the reference alongside high protein and fat: the basket is meat- and dairy-heavy rather than grain-heavy.
